# NGC 4236 pilot, tests specified before measuring Euclid counterparts

Question: are GALEX FUV knots away from the projected midplane accompanied by compact optical structure in Euclid VIS? The literature reports off-plane H-alpha emission but UV or optical morphology alone does not demonstrate a three-dimensional height or membership in NGC 4236.

Adopt the SIMBAD centre (184.175492, 69.462569 deg) and literature major-axis PA 162 deg. Search |major| ≤480 arcsec, 60 ≤ |minor| ≤180 arcsec, in FUV usable exposure >500 s. Detect 6 arcsec minimum-separated peaks above five times the MAD-derived noise of a filtered FUV map. Subtract a smooth local background and require a corresponding NUV excess above three times its filtered background noise within 3 arcsec. Filtered-noise multiples are screening thresholds, not calibrated detection probabilities.

Measure the strongest compact Euclid VIS excess within 2 arcsec of each UV coordinate, relative to a 3–5 arcsec local annulus. Use equal search radii at control coordinates 15, 30 and 45 arcsec to either side along the projected major axis, dropping controls outside the search box, with inadequate image coverage or within 8 arcsec of any selected UV peak. Use median of the locally background-normalized optical statistic at each set of controls, report individual locations, and inspect candidate and control cutouts. Reject optical measurements with less than 90% usable coverage inside a 5 arcsec circle. Carry out an explicit estimate of accidental UV–VIS alignments through these local controls. Express all offsets as projected distances. Treat the pilot as inconclusive whenever the archival data leave membership, foreground sources, or projection unresolved.
