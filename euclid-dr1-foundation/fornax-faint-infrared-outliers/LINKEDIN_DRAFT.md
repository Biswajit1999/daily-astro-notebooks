Day [number] out of 365 Earthian Days — Do faint infrared outliers keep their unusual colours?

I searched a small patch of Euclid Deep Field Fornax for faint objects with colours often associated with cool brown dwarfs. Out of 1,413 sources that passed the quality cuts, 28 entered the colour box. I checked the same sources with a second photometry method. Thirteen stayed inside the box, and none passed the predefined uncertainty test.

Independent telescope images from Blanco/DECam and measurements from VISTA show sources at the three highest-ranked positions. Their VISTA colours were less blue through a different set of filters, and one target lies near the glare of a bright star. Their identities remain open.
