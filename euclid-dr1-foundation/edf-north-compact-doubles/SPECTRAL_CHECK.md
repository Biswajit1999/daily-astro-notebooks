# Exploratory spectral checks · 18 November 2026

Written after seeing the images and catalogue redshift proposals, before inspecting the SIR flux arrays. Extract both released NISP grism coadds by exact MER ID and tile for the top six systems. Keep mask bit counts, overlap and sources with no spectrum. Treat two component extractions separated by less than 1 arcsec as potentially contaminated by one another, even when both return similar redshifts.

Evaluate fixed vacuum rest wavelengths [O II] 3727.42 Å (effective blend), Hβ 4862.68 Å, [O III] 5008.24 Å, Hα 6564.61 Å, and [S II] 6718.29 Å at the rank-zero SPE galaxy redshift for each source. Fit a straight continuum and a Gaussian of 16 Å sigma within ±120 Å, with inverse-variance weights and mask = 0. Require at least four unmasked bins within ±32 Å and at least 12 sideband bins at |Δλ| ≥ 42 Å. Inflate uncertainties if reduced χ² exceeds one. Record signed S/N, grism and wavelength insecurities. A positive line test above 4 formal σ is a diagnostic only. A fixed-center calculation from an estimate derived from the same spectrum is not an independent significance test. Explore Hα/[O III]/[O II] alternatives for any isolated feature. Do not adopt a component redshift unless at least two credible features or independent matching spectroscopy support it and slitless overlap does not dominate the inference.
