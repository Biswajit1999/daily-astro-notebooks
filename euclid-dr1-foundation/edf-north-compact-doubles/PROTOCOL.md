# Preregistration · 18 November 2026

## Subarcsecond two-peak sources in the Euclid Deep Field North

**Question.** Does a blind VIS image search in six fixed DR1-Foundation MER tile centres find any candidate with two peaks that repeat in the NISP J mosaic at the same positions?

The fixed footprint is the central 6,144 × 6,144 native pixels in each of MER deep tiles 101829775–101829780, ordered by tile ID. Use the nominal 0.1 arcsec pixel grid and record WCS centres, exclusions, and actual unique sky footprint. Operate on released background-subtracted mosaics. Only the deeper DR1 product for each tile and band is eligible. Keep absent or unusable products and completed tiles in the manifest. Run all six tiles regardless of the outcome.

Use a classical difference of Gaussians with pixel sigma 0.85 and 3.0 on VIS. Evaluate both signs as a diagnostic. Use 512-pixel background cells and a 1.4826-scaled MAD of the filtered image for the local score. Peaks must be local 5 × 5 maxima and score at least 8 and 6, with the brighter peak ranked by filtered amplitude. Require integer-pixel peak separation 0.38–0.95 arcsec, a fainter-to-brighter amplitude ratio ≥0.25, and a saddle depression ≥15% of the fainter amplitude along the segment. Exclude pairs within 14 pixels of invalid or zero coverage, within 7 arcsec of any >1,000σ peak in the corresponding signed map, or with a third ≥6σ peak within 1.5 arcsec of the midpoint. Exclude a 16-pixel border. Keep rejected seed pairs, flags, and negative controls. Report signed-map counts and use the closest midpoint within 0.3 arcsec to flag duplicates, if any. These are image-domain contrasts in correlated noise, not Gaussian detection probabilities.

At every retained VIS pair, measure the signed NISP-J matched-filter map using sigma 1.25 and 3.5 pixels and a local median/MAD in a 20–65 pixel annulus. Require two distinct J maxima within 0.16 arcsec of the VIS positions, both ≥4 local robust sigma, with a ≥10% saddle depression and the separation ≥0.25 arcsec. The same test with the two positions shifted 3 arcsec north is an empirical positional control. Preserve failed or absent checks. Report counts and a Wilson 95% interval for the fraction of testable VIS pairs recovered in J. The interval describes the selected sample. Differences in filter, depth and PSF limit completeness. VIS and NISP are two instruments aboard Euclid.

Rank candidates by the weaker J peak score among repeaters, then by the weaker VIS score, then coordinates. Inspect up to six in registered Subaru HSC i2, or a public Legacy r cutout if HSC is unavailable at the position. An external image that only shows a blended source supplies positional support, not a two-component confirmation. Check WDS, Gaia DR3, SIMBAD and available HST/JWST footprints or higher-resolution images. Record angular resolution, local depth proxy, masks, detector defects, bright halos and nearby third sources. Crossmatch released MER/PHZ/SPE when supported by the archive. Astrometric or redshift claims require matching and quality evidence. A repeatable double peak could be two galaxies, clumps in one galaxy, a foreground/background alignment, stars, or lens images. Claim neither a new object nor a physical association from images alone. For a secure galaxy redshift, use Planck18 for distances and propagate the redshift interval.
