# Euclid DR1-Foundation research

Biswajit Jana

| Study | Data and result | Report |
|---|---|---|
| [Hα measurements and optical aperture sizes in Euclid Deep Field North](edf-north-halpha/) | MER VIS imaging, NISP slitless spectra and DESI redshifts. The direct VIS size contrast spans zero. Fourteen warning-inclusive dual-grism detections yield five candidates that survive peak-bin removal. | [PDF](edf-north-halpha/report/Euclid_EDF_North_Halpha.pdf) |
| [Ultraviolet knots and optical counterparts in NGC 4236](ngc4236-uv-optical/) | Euclid VIS, GALEX FUV and NUV, Gaia DR3, and SINGS imaging. Gaia identifies one foreground optical counterpart. The narrow-band and R-band images both cover 10 of the 23 exterior candidates, and the study leaves their connection to the extraplanar gas unresolved. | [PDF](ngc4236-uv-optical/report/NGC4236_UV_VIS.pdf) |
| [Can Euclid colours distinguish the near-redshift arcs of the Carousel Lens?](carousel-lens-colours/) | VIS and NISP imaging gives a nominal Y−H contrast of −0.184 ± 0.112 mag for the distinct-source pair. A same-source pair shows a comparable contrast, and the true faint counterimage fails the nominal infrared cut. | [Research note](carousel-lens-colours/) |

Each new study starts with a distinct question and a different dataset, field, structure, or sample. Its report includes an observed target or field image, a single prose abstract, and links to measurements and reproduction code.

The [first field pilot](2026-11-14-day01/), [eight-tile checks](2026-11-15-day02/), and [grism-overlap tests](day03-cross-grism/) are archived components of the EDF North Hα study.
